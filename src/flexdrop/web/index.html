What the student sees

  1. Order as usual. FlexDrop predicts preparation and travel time and shows the standard ETA.
  2. Choose flexible delivery (optional). When nearby orders make coordination likely, the student is offered a small reward for a few extra minutes. The reward is priced from expected savings.
  3. Consent is explicit. The delivery window changes only after the student accepts. The optimiser then uses as little of that flexibility as possible.
